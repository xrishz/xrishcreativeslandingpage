# Our Works

Mode: Experience. Scope: /works.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is the work itself—four clear event chapters, each with video that plays without leaving the XRISH site.

OWN-WORLD: Warm gallery paper for personal celebrations, screening black for Corporate Events and Graduation, oversized chapter type, thin rules and full-width embedded players without card shells.

STORY: OUR WORKS opens a four-item index: DEBUTS, PREDEBUTS, CORPORATE EVENTS, and GRADUATION. Debut and Predebut use the two confirmed Facebook players. No external film actions are shown. Each playable film starts with an explicit Play film surface; activation mounts its iframe on this page. Loading film remains visible until the iframe load event, with a Try again action after a 15-second timeout. The four supplied Drive masters were removed after real browser playback failed. Corporate Events and Graduation show restrained preparation lines until web-ready films are available. Graduation keeps a two-column desktop film area ready for two; at 700px and below it becomes one column.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The two-line OUR WORKS title and four ruled chapter links make the archive understandable before scrolling.

SIGNATURE: The light-to-dark transition turns the lower half of the archive into a restrained screening room while keeping the player layout editorial and direct.
